## Overview

## What I Did & Learned
- **Theme Customization**: Customized an existing Jekyll theme to better fit my style and content layout.
- **Tech Stack Used**:

  - **HTML/CSS & Markdown**: To structure content smoothly, including my resume, SQLD/AI-POT certifications, and study notes.
  - **Git & GitHub**: For version control and deployment.
